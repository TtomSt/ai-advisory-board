## KNOW-001

**Date:** 2026-06-05

**Type:** Market

**Title:** Multi-AI power user workflow is real and established by 2026

**Source:** Founder observation + AIPOS Onboarding Sprint

**Summary:** By 2026, advanced AI users routinely work across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and DeepSeek. Many already compare outputs, cross-check answers, and move context between systems manually. The workflow (Question → AI A → copy → AI B → copy → AI C → compare → decision) is an established practice among knowledge workers, researchers, consultants, and AI practitioners. This is a market observation, not a product hypothesis.

**Key Insight:** The existence of multi-AI users is not the business risk. The business risk is whether they experience the manual orchestration as painful enough to change their behavior.

**Relevance to current work:** Informed the reframing of HYP-001 from "do these users exist?" to "do they feel enough friction to want it removed?" Prevented Phase 1 from validating an already-answered question.

**Linked entries:**

- KNOW-002 — the conceptual principle this observation illustrates

## KNOW-002

**Date:** 2026-06-05

**Type:** Concept

**Title:** Waste existence is not the same as waste pain

**Source:** AIPOS Onboarding Sprint — Thinking Caps — 2026-06-05

**Summary:** A Lean practitioner can observe and correctly identify waste in a process — non-value-adding work, unnecessary motion, manual transfer of information — and yet the users performing that work may not experience it as painful enough to motivate behavior change. The existence of waste is an observation. The desire to eliminate it is a validated hypothesis. These are different things and must not be conflated when building products.

In Polish: Istnienie marnotrawstwa nie oznacza jeszcze, że użytkownik odczuwa je jako problem wart rozwiązania.

**Key Insight:** Identifying waste is not enough to prove product demand. The critical question is not "does this waste exist?" but "does the user feel enough friction to change their workflow and pay for a solution?" A Lean practitioner sees waste. A product builder must validate that users feel it as pain.

**Relevance to current work:** Directly reframed the Thinking Caps Phase 1 validation strategy. The original HYP-001 tested whether multi-AI power users exist — a market observation already answered by 2026. The reframed HYP-001 tests whether those users experience the friction as painful enough to want it removed. The distinction prevented Phase 1 from validating the wrong thing.

**Linked entries:**

- HYP-002 (Thinking Caps) — behavioral change hypothesis that follows from this
- HYP-003 (Thinking Caps) — commercial validation that depends on pain being real
- KNOW-001 — the specific market observation this principle applies to
